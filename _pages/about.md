My research focuses on bird movement ecology and conservation.

I use GPS tracking and remote sensing imagery to investigate how birds use and select habitats across their annual cycle. My work aims to understand how habitat quality, availability and landscape structure influence shorebird movements and habitat use, with the broader goal of informing land management and conservation.
