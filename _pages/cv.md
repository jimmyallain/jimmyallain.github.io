Responsible for monitoring populations of seabirds and marine mammals. Field activities included bird ringing, GLS/GPS tracking, transponder tagging, blood sampling and population censuses.

Participation in a field campaign involving long-term monitoring of a population of Black Redstarts. Activities included GLS deployment, bird ringing, bioacoustic monitoring and database management.

Study of reproductive senescence in chipmunks in relation to age at primiparity. Responsibilities included statistical modelling and analyses in R, as well as database preparation using longitudinal capture–mark–recapture (CMR) data.

Fieldwork included bird ringing, blood sampling for DNA analyses and blood smear preparation, and biometric measurements. Statistical analyses were also conducted.
