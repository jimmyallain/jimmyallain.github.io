### "Tuit-tuit": an endangered endemic forest bird

The Tuit-tuit (*Lalage newtoni*) is a Critically Endangered passerine endemic to Réunion Island. It is restricted to native high-altitude forests (1,000–1,800 m) in the north-western part of the island.

{% include figure.html path="assets/img/projects/tuittuit.JPG" title="Tuit-tuit (*Lalage newtoni*) © Jaime Martinez." class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" %}

**Main threats**

Introduced rats and feral cats are major threats to the species, preying on eggs and chicks and contributing to nest failure.

**Conservation actions and monitoring**

Annual conservation programmes combine rat control campaigns with the monitoring of breeding territories and pairs. Field surveys across forest massifs are conducted over several days to locate singing males and identify active nests. Repeated visits to monitored territories make it possible to assess breeding success, notably by recording fledglings leaving their nests.

This work was carried out at the SEOR (Reunion Island Ornithological Society).

During my stay on the island, I also helped out at the wildlife rescue center with missions to rescue seabirds (Barau's Petrel, Mascarene Petrel, Shearwater, etc.).
